CONCEPT MISSION // MARS

GROW LIFE ON MARS.

ARES is a conceptual closed-loop Mars aquaponics habitat that turns Martian ice into a usable water resource, explores biological purification using engineered microorganisms, and uses the recovered water to support fish, plants and human life inside an inflatable pressurized habitat.

02 // MARTIAN ICE

Water starts with the ice.

Instead of assuming an imported water supply, ARES explores the use of Martian water ice as a local resource. The ice would be excavated, melted and processed before entering biological systems.

MARTIAN ICE RESOURCE // CONCEPT
ICE → MELTING → PRE-TREATMENT → PURIFICATION
🧊

Ice Extraction

ARES assumes access to a suitable Martian water-ice deposit. Extraction would need to operate under extreme cold, low pressure and abrasive dust conditions.

🔥

Controlled Melting

Recovered ice is converted into liquid water using controlled thermal energy before entering downstream treatment systems.

🧬

Biological Treatment

Engineered microorganisms are proposed as a research-stage purification component that could help transform or remove selected contaminants under controlled conditions.

🧪

Final Water Treatment

Biological treatment would not replace safety controls. Filtration, chemical treatment, monitoring and final water-quality verification would remain critical before water enters the aquaponics loop.

03 // BIO-PURIFICATION

Biology becomes part of the filter.

ARES explores the use of engineered microorganisms as a controlled biological treatment stage. The concept is to design microbial systems that can interact with selected contaminants or transform target compounds before water reaches the sensitive aquaponics ecosystem.

ENGINEERED BIOFILTER CONCEPTUAL RESEARCH STAGE
🧬 Targeted Biology

Microorganisms would be designed and selected around specific treatment goals rather than acting as an uncontrolled ecosystem.

🔒 Containment

The biological treatment stage would require physical containment and monitoring so engineered organisms do not enter the wider habitat system.

🔬 Verify Before Use

Water would require downstream testing before being considered suitable for aquaponics, agriculture or human use.

04 // AQUAPONICS

The heart of ARES.

Once water has passed the necessary treatment stages, it enters the aquaponics ecosystem. Fish provide nutrients, microorganisms process waste, plants absorb nutrients and the water is treated and circulated again.

AQUATIC SYSTEM

🐟 Fish produce nutrients

Fish consume feed and produce organic waste. In a controlled aquaponics system, that waste becomes part of the nutrient cycle rather than simply being discarded.

BIOLOGICAL CYCLING

🦠 Microbes transform nutrients

Beneficial microorganisms convert nitrogenous compounds into forms that plants can use. This biological process is separate from the proposed Martian-water purification stage.

FOOD PRODUCTION

🌱 Plants clean & produce

Plants absorb nutrients from the circulating water while producing edible biomass for the habitat.

RESOURCE RECOVERY

♻️ Water returns to the loop

After appropriate filtration and monitoring, water is recirculated. The objective is to minimize losses while maintaining biological stability.

05 // INFLATABLE HABITAT

A protected bubble on Mars.

ARES uses a conceptual inflatable pressurized habitat as the primary living environment. The aquaponics system sits inside this protected volume, allowing food production and life-support infrastructure to operate together.

HABITAT 01

🎈 Inflatable Pressure Shell

The concept uses a deployable inflatable architecture to create a large internal pressurized volume while reducing launch packaging requirements.

HABITAT 02

🛡️ External Protection

An operational system would still require shielding against radiation, micrometeoroids and the harsh Martian environment, potentially using regolith or additional protective layers.

HABITAT 03

🌱 Aquaponics Inside

The inflatable volume houses the aquaponics tanks, plant-growing systems, water-treatment equipment and crew work areas.

HABITAT 04

🚪 Controlled Access

Airlocks separate the pressurized habitat from the external Martian environment and help reduce the loss of atmosphere.

08 // ENGINEERING CHALLENGES

Mars is the hard part.

ARES is a conceptual engineering project. The biggest challenge is making every subsystem work reliably together under Martian conditions.

🧊

Martian Ice

Ice deposits may be difficult to access and extraction requires significant energy.

→ Resource mapping + efficient extraction

🧬

Biological Purification

Engineered microorganisms would need to be stable, contained and demonstrably effective against target contaminants.

→ Controlled bioreactors + verification

🐟

Aquaponics Stability

Fish, plants and microorganisms create a tightly interconnected biological system.

→ Sensors + redundancy + isolation

☢️

Radiation

Mars provides much less atmospheric protection than Earth.

→ Shielding + habitat protection

🎈

Inflatable Habitat

A large pressurized inflatable structure must tolerate pressure loads, abrasion, impacts and the Martian environment.

→ Layered shell + protective covering

⚡

Power

Melting ice, pumping water, lighting crops, heating and life support all require reliable energy.

→ Primary power + storage + redundancy

♻️ Resource Efficiency

Recover water and nutrients wherever practical.

🛡️ Redundancy

Critical life-support and water systems should not rely on one point of failure.

🔬 Evidence First

Clearly separate measured data, simulations, assumptions and future research concepts.

09 // MISSION

Turn ice into water.
Turn water into life.

ARES explores a possible future where Martian ice becomes a local water resource, engineered biology assists purification, aquaponics produces food, and an inflatable habitat gives the entire system a protected place to operate.
